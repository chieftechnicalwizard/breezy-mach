Pair New Coleman-Mach Unit

  1. Stand near the Coleman-Mach physical thermostat.
  2. Press & hold BOTH the UP and DOWN buttons for 5 seconds.
  3. The thermostat screen will flash its 6-digit Unit ID (e.g. 96 89 00).
  4. Enter the 6-digit PIN below and click Start Pairing.
